TRANSFORMERS


a transformer is an attention based neural network architures that process all words in parallel and learns relationships btw them ChatGPT , gemini uses transformers

goal:

read the entire sentence at once and figure out which words should pay attention 

eg: the trophy didn't fit in the bag because it was too big

it -->trophy(0.8)/bag(0.2)

why transformers where introduced?
before transformers models like RNN,LSTM and biLSTM processed text word by word in sequence 

problems with older models:

*slow training
*hard to remember long sentences
*difficult to do the parallel processing 
*performance drops on long sentences/text

these problems are solved using attention mechanism  

eg: the transformer used in chatbots, language translation ,summerizing speech, code generation

ENCODER

an encoder is a part of transformers that reads the complete i\p text, understands the meaning using context , and converts it into numerical representation(vectors)

it doesn't generate text it is mainly used for understanding the text 

models for the encoder --> BERT(GOOGLE),ROBERTA(META it is better than BERT ), DistillBERT(hungging face),ALBERT(GOOGLE lightweight to other models),ELECTRA(GOOGLE),DEBERTa(MICROSOFT it has high accuracy),XLM(META it is used for multillungal preposes)
